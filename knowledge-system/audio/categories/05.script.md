Sanjay here. The AI infrastructure story is no longer one curve, it is two parallel curves, and the cheaper one matters more for us.

This category tracks the structural compute, protocol, and energy bets being placed today that will define the next five years.

First, the efficiency revolution. DeepSeek trained a frontier-class reasoner for roughly five point six million dollars, while comparable Western models cost ten to twenty times more. Pre-training efficiency improves around three times per year. The compute moat is quietly eroding.

Second, inference is collapsing toward zero. GPT four equivalent performance now runs at about forty cents per million tokens, down from roughly twenty dollars three years ago. Vera Rubin promises another tenfold cut. Embedded agents become economically trivial.

Third, MCP is becoming the HTTP of AI. Ten thousand active servers, ninety-seven million monthly downloads, governed by the Linux Foundation, every major provider on board. Forrester predicts thirty percent of enterprise SaaS vendors ship MCP servers this year.

Fourth, energy is the binding constraint. Data center electricity surged seventeen percent in twenty twenty-five. Forty-five gigawatts of small modular reactor agreements sit on paper, but none yet powers a production cluster. The gap through twenty thirty is real.

Net read: scale maximalism funds the labs, but efficiency plus protocols funds the enterprise.

If your work is enterprise AI strategy: stop optimizing for cost-per-query, design for inference abundance, treat MCP as core plumbing.
