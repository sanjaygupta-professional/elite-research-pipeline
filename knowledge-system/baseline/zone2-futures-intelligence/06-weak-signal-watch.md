**Last updated:** April 2026

## What This Category Is

A running log of weak signals — ideas, research, observations that are:
- Low confidence now
- Directionally important if they prove out
- Not yet corroborated by multiple sources

**Rule:** Do not amplify these publicly. Track them. When a weak signal gets corroborated by a second independent credible source, consider upgrading it to a strong signal and moving it to the relevant Zone 1 category.

**Scoring note:** Weak signals by definition have low Evidence Strength (1-2) but may score high on Capability Unlock (3-4). The combination of low evidence + high potential unlock is exactly what this category exists to track.

**Source:** University of California AI research (2026), research papers
**Profile:** E2 T-Emerging U3 H-Grounded Z-Medium
**Why it matters:** Internal "mumbling" combined with short-term memory helps AI adapt to tasks outside training distribution. If validated, this suggests a path to more robust, self-improving AI systems without additional training data — directly addressing the data exhaustion constraint.
**What to watch for:** Whether this is replicated by a major AI lab and incorporated into a frontier model release.

**[April 2026] | Post-training phase overtakes pre-training as primary capability driver**
**Source:** MIT Technology Review ("What's next for AI in 2026"), InfoWorld
**Profile:** E3 T-Shifting U3 H-Grounded Z-Now
**Why it matters:** The era of building ever-larger foundation models is hitting limits — established scaling laws are showing diminishing returns and high-quality pre-training data is running out. The biggest capability improvements now come from post-training: RLHF, distillation, chain-of-thought training, instruction tuning. This shifts competitive advantage from "who has the most compute" to "who has the best post-training methodology."
**What to watch for:** Whether the next generation of frontier models (GPT-5, Claude 5) show more improvement from post-training innovations than from scale increases. If so, the scaling maximalist thesis weakens further.
**Status:** WATCHING — close to upgrade

**Cluster assessment:** Multiple signals suggesting that current safety assurance approaches are becoming insufficient as model capability increases. The cluster is small but high-consequence.
**If the cluster upgrades:** Governance (Category 04) shifts from compliance focus to continuous monitoring focus. Enterprise AI deployment requires new safety architectures.
