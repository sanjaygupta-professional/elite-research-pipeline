# Knowledge Taxonomy

## The Signal Classification Lens
*Applied to every item, across every category, before it enters the system*

```
STRONG SIGNAL    Multiple credible sources confirm · Real deployment · Measurable outcomes
                 → Consume now · Form POV · Eligible for publishing

WEAK SIGNAL      One credible source · Conceptually sound · Low confidence but directional
                 → Track · Watch for corroboration · Do not amplify yet

NOISE            Vendor hype · Echo chamber · Media repeating itself · No new information
                 → Discard · Do not let it steal attention
```

This classification applies horizontally — every item in every zone gets tagged before processing.

## 4 Zones, 10 Categories

### Zone 1: Present Intelligence
*What is real and happening now. Strong signals only.*  
**Feeds:** "Here's what's actually happening" — practitioner LinkedIn, Accenture advisory, client briefs

| # | Category | Core question | Signal type |
|---|----------|--------------|-------------|
| 1 | **GenAI Capabilities** | What can AI actually do now — benchmarks, real deployments, not announcements | Strong only |
| 2 | **Enterprise AI & Org Transformation** | How are organizations genuinely restructuring, governing, and deploying AI | Strong + emerging |
| 3 | **Workforce & Human-AI Collaboration** | Skill shifts, role changes, team redesign — with evidence | Strong + emerging |
| 4 | **AI Governance & Ethics** | Policy, regulation, trust frameworks gaining real traction | Strong + weak |

### Zone 2: Futures Intelligence
*Where the long-arc POV is built. Your most differentiated territory.*  
**Feeds:** "Here's where I think we're going and why" — scenario essays, keynotes, DBA research, "Possibilities with Probabilities" at its fullest

| # | Category | Core question | Signal type |
|---|----------|--------------|-------------|
| 5 | **AI Infrastructure Trajectory** | What is being built at the foundation — compute curves, model capability roadmaps, emerging protocols (MCP, agent standards), major lab bets | Weak → Strong watch |
| 6 | **Weak Signal Watch** | Early research, fringe ideas, contrarian voices, PhD-level experiments. Low confidence now — but watching for corroboration | Weak only |
| 7 | **Long-Arc Futures (POV Construction)** | Synthesis layer: given the infrastructure being built, what does 2028–2032 look like? This is Sanjay's stake in the ground | OUTPUT — not input |

**Note on Category 7:** This is not consumed content — it is *produced* content. It is synthesized from Categories 5 and 6 plus everything in Zone 1. Updated quarterly as the evidence base evolves.

## How the Zones Connect

```
Zone 4 (Lab)              empirical grounding from hands-on experiments
Zone 3 (Toolkit)          practitioner credibility from daily tool use
Zone 1 (Present)          what's real and evidenced today
Zone 2 (Futures)          where we're going — built on evidence from all other zones
                                    ↓
                       "Possibilities with Probabilities"
              Present-grounded · Futures-oriented · Practitioner-tested
                                    ↓
         LinkedIn · DBA Research · Accenture TL · Client Keynotes · Newsletter
```

**The key compounding effect:** Zone 4 experimentation grounds Zone 2 futures scenarios in technical reality. An executive who has run a local agent can make a much more credible claim about what agentic AI means for org structure than one who has only read about it.
