Sanjay here. This week, the AI-as-workflow story stopped being theoretical.

Bun, the JavaScript runtime, rewrote its entire codebase in eleven days using AI assistance. The API cost was roughly one hundred and sixty-five thousand dollars. That is equivalent to one year of small-team engineering. Three independent sources confirmed the timeline and cost. OpenAI launched ChatGPT Work the same week, the first general-purpose product designed to run full knowledge-work loops autonomously over hours. Dan Shipper at Every named the shift plainly: your role becomes tending the system, not doing the work.

Meta adds the infrastructure layer. SemiAnalysis reports Meta is building five compute clusters each exceeding one gigawatt. Alongside that, a three-thousand-engineer team records employee workflows and converts them into model training tasks. The organization is the training data.

Anthropic's J-Space interpretability paper reveals the interior: a verbalizable global workspace inside Claude, where roughly six to twenty-five concepts are active simultaneously and externally steerable. This is a governance advance. It is also an adversarial surface.
